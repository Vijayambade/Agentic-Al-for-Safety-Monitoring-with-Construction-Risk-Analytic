# Agentic AI Safety Monitoring Construction Risk Analytics

Construction Management Dashboard developed using **Python** and **Streamlit**.

Agentic AI Safety Monitoring Construction Risk Analytics is designed to simplify construction project management by providing a centralized platform for project tracking, material estimation, analytics, AI-assisted insights, reporting, and construction knowledge.

---

## Project Overview

Construction projects involve multiple stakeholders, large volumes of data, material planning, progress tracking, and safety management. Traditional methods often lead to delays, miscommunication, and inefficient decision-making.

Agentic AI Safety Monitoring Construction Risk Analytics provides an interactive dashboard that helps engineers, contractors, and project managers monitor projects, estimate construction materials, generate reports, and access construction knowledge from a single platform.
